## 3. MONEY: How to Afford Gear Upgrades

### Fastest Gold/Hour Methods (at Combat Lvl 50)

#### Option A: Cow Hides + Tanning
- **Rate**: ~50-60 hides/hour = 2,500-3,000 gp/hr
- **Mechanics**: Kill cows, loot hides, bank them, sell in bulk
- **Profit**: Hides sell for ~30-50 gp each when stacked
- **Limitation**: Cows drop mixed loot; hides are random

#### Option B: Bone Looting (Ground Items)
- **Rate**: ~20-30 big bones/hour = 1,500-2,250 gp/hr (if you find them)
- **Risk**: Bones are dropped by killed NPCs; requires active harvesting
- **Note**: Hill Giants drop big bones (15 XP each for Prayer, valuable)

#### Option C: Thieving Stalls (if you have Thieving skill)
- **Rate**: 4,000+ gp/hr at high levels
- **Current Issue**: You're likely not trained in Thieving

#### Option D: Fishing → Cooking → Selling
- **Rate**: ~1,500-2,000 gp/hr (slower but passive)
- **Mechanics**: Fish shrimp at Draynor, cook, sell as food

### Recommended Path: Cow Hides
- You're already at cows grinding Defence
- Loot hides as you kill, deposit in bank every 5 minutes
- Use dropped hides to fund gear upgrades
- **Target**: 5,000 gp for Steel Scimitar = ~2-3 hours of focused hide looting

### Early Money Stash
- Check your bank; you have 25 gp already
- Kill 100 cows = ~50 hides = 1,500-2,500 gp
- That's enough for Steel Scimitar at 50 Attack
