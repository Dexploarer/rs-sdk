# Routes & Danger Zones

Hard-won knowledge about getting around safely.

## Danger Zones

### Draynor Village — Dark Wizards
- **Location**: South of Draynor bank, around (3085-3095, 3225-3235)
- **Level**: 7 (magic attacks, hit through low Defence)
- **Aggressive**: YES — they attack on sight
- **Risk**: LETHAL for low Defence characters. With Defence 1, they can kill you in 10-15 hits.
- **Learned**: Dexrunner nearly died walking from cow field to Draynor bank. Arrived at 14/41 HP.
- **Mitigation**:
  - Approach bank from the NORTH (through Lumbridge, then west along road)
  - Or carry food and eat while running through
  - Never walk through at low HP

### Lumbridge Swamp — Giant Spiders
- **Location**: South of Lumbridge
- **Aggressive**: Some are
- **Risk**: Low-medium

### Wilderness (north of Edgeville)
- **PvP zone**: Other players can attack you
- **Never enter without preparation**

## Safe Routes

### Cow Field → Draynor Bank (SAFE ROUTE)
- From cows (3253, 3290): walk WEST to road, then SOUTH along road
- Avoid cutting south through fields (Dark Wizard territory)
- Alternative: Walk north to Varrock West Bank (3185, 3436) — longer but zero danger

### Lumbridge → Al Kharid
- Through toll gate (10gp fee)
- Or go around through Lumbridge Swamp (free but longer and spiders)

### Lumbridge → Varrock
- Follow road north — completely safe, no aggressive monsters on path

## Pre-Travel Checklist
1. Check HP — if below 50%, eat food first or wait to regen
2. Check inventory — have food for emergencies (minimum 3-5 pieces)
3. Check route — are there aggressive monsters along the way?
4. Check combat state — make sure you're not already in combat before moving

## Recovery Rules
- If HP drops below 30% during travel: STOP, eat food, reassess
- If being attacked by multiple enemies: RUN (walkTo a safe spot), don't fight
- If no food and low HP: stay at bank/safe area until HP regenerates
- HP regenerates ~1 per minute naturally (faster with Rapid Heal prayer at level 22)
